## How I work

1. Clarify the business outcome and constraints.
2. Convert ambiguity into decisions, scope and acceptance criteria.
3. Break delivery into small, traceable work items.
4. Validate architecture, security and operational assumptions early.
5. Use AI and automation where they improve speed without weakening control.
6. Verify the whole flow, document decisions and prepare the product for handover or release.
